I am a first-year Computer Science Master's student at **École Normale Supérieure de Rennes**.

My coursework covers a wide landscape of theoretical and systems computer science, including high-performance computing, operating systems, bioinformatics, category theory, and interactive program proving.

## Research Interests

My primary research focus lies in **concurrent programming**. 

I initiated my research journey during an undergraduate internship at INRIA / IRISA (WIDE team), supervised by Achour Mostefaoui, where I learned about non-blocking synchronization for concurrent data structures in a shared-memory model. This year, I am continuing to explore this area through a year-long research project.
